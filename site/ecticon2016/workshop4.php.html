Title : "Quantum cryptography updating from China: USTC & Qasky's QKD live network"

Abstract

Quantum key distribution is developing quickly in China. Some big projects are implemented, such as Jing-Hu main line, Hu-Hang main line and quantum satellite. Commercial users mostly care about security, stability and convenient. Qasky company has done much on these three points. Here I will give an introduction about our product and project.

Organized by Dr.Yi-Bo Zhao, GM, Anhui Qasky Technology Co. Ltd.
