  ECTI Workshop

Title : "How to write and revise scientific paper and get it published"
Abstract

This ECTI workshop aims to provide some vital information for authors who prepare the manuscripts for international journals. The mind of reviewers and the important ingredients of the good manuscripts will be shared. Finally “practicing under the mind of a reviewer” shall conclude the workshop.

Note : Interested persons can be notified via an email (ecticon2016@gmail.com) or walk in at ECTI-CON 2016 in JUNE 28th,2016.
