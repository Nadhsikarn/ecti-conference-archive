Welcome Message

Dear Esteemed Colleagues and Supporters,

On behalf of the Organizing Committee, We would like to extend a warm and friendly invitation for your participation and contribution to the 2021 IEEE 18th International Conference on Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ECTI-CON 2021), which will be held on May 19-22, 2021 at the Empress Hotel in Chiang Mai, Thailand.

ECTI-CON 2021 follows the tradition of premium technical quality as one of the leading IEEE conferences in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology, sponsored by the ECTI Association Thailand, cooperated by the IEEE Thailand Section, and hosted by Chiang Mai University. The conference will cover tutorials, keynote speeches and technical sessions including oral presentation, test and applications related to Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology from industry track. It is our pleasure to welcome you to ECTI-CON 2021.

We are looking forward to meeting you in Chiang Mai, Thailand.

With our best regards,

General Chair, ECTI-CON2021
Assoc.Prof.Dr.Yuttana Kumsuwan
(Chiang Mai University, Thailand)
