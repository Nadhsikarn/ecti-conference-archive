Student Travel Grants

Purpose

The Student Travel Grant aims at providing financial support to students in order to encourage their professional growth as future leaders in the field of electrical engineering, electronics, computer, telecommunications and information technology.

Grant overview

The grant is meant to support personal attendance to a number of students at the ECTI-CON 2021 conference. Students selected for the grant will receive free registration and 5000THB travel expenses. Grantees need to pay for and make their own accommodation arrangements. The exact number of the awards will be determined based on the number of eligible applicants and the availability of funds.

Requirements for Grant consideration

Successful applicants must present their papers at the ECTI-CON 2021 Technical Sessions.

Application Procedure

Students are encouraged to have the applications sent as soon as possible. Applications arriving after the deadline will not be considered. The student travel grant application deadline is April 10, 2021.

Please, click here to download the ECTICON2021 application form.
Note: Email the application form in PDF format to the conference secretariat (ecticon2021@eng.cmu.ac.th) as an attachment, with an email subject line: ECTI_GRANT_ [PAPER ID].
