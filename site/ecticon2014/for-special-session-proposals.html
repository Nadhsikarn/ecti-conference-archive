Call for Special Session Proposals

 

 

 Download template of special session proposal

     The aim of a special session is to provide researchers with an opportunity to present their latest, cutting-edge research within specific fields relevant to the theme of the conference.

 

Organizing a Special Session

            Participants interested in organizing a special session as part of ECTI-CON 2014 are requested to submit a proposal to the conference organizers by September 30, 2013. Prospective session organizers should consider the following items prior to submitting the proposal:

Preparation of the Proposal

            The Special Session Organizer(s) should provide the following information:

Title: Describe the subject of the session as accurately and specifically as possible in not more than 10 words.

Organizer: Provide the full name, title, affiliation, address, email address, telephone number, and, when available, fax number.

Summary: Describe the session in not more than 100 words. The summary should be written to attract those who specifically work on the topic as well as those who work in related areas. Based on experience, the following outline is suggested: 

         - Describe the scope and the aim of the session.

        - Explain the problem area to be addressed by the speakers and why it is important.

        - Identify current directions of research and methods being developed to solve problems.

Prospective papers: Provide a list of prospective papers (4 to 6 papers), indicating principal author, title of papers and the affiliation of the principal author.  

The template for the proposal can be downloaded. (Click here)
