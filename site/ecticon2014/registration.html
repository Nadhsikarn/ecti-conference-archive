Registration & Camera Ready Paper Submission

 

 

 

Registration Information:        

“Registration fees are non refundable and non transferable.”

         "IEEE reserves the right to exclude a paper from distribution after the conference, including IEEE Xplore® Digital Library, if the paper is not presented at the conference."

1.   Each accepted paper needs at least 1 regular or ECTI/IEEE Member or ECTI/IEEE Student Member early-bird registration; otherwise the paper cannot be included in the conference proceedings. Note that ONE author registration will cover only ONE paper.

2.  At least one author of each accepted paper MUST present paper in the conference in order      for the paper to be included in the proceedings. 

3.   Member or Student Member of ECTI or IEEE must declare a valid membership status e.g. a copy of Member or Student Member card or other membership evidences upon registration, where our registration will allow a file download of relevant membership evidences to the system.

4.   The author(s) pays the registration fee by logging into the author account and selecting payment. The author(s) can pay for one accepted paper at a time. (Entering Camera Ready Paper Submission & Author-Presenter Registration Website)

5.   For other authors (not presenting papers) or observers, please enter the registration site here. 

 

Remark:

1.   Regular, ECTI/IEEE Member, ECTI/IEEE Student Member Registration covers Abstract book, CD-ROM, Bag, Souvenir, Coffee-Break, Lunch, Welcome Reception (May 14), Banquet (May 15).

2.   Accompanying person registration covers Abstract book, CD-ROM, Bag, Souvenir, Coffee-Break, Lunch, Welcome Reception (May 14), Banquet (May 15). But accompanying person registration cannot cover the inclusion of papers into IEEEXplore.

3.   Additional ticket for Welcome Reception on May 14 is 700 THB (available at the registration desk)

4.   Additional ticket for banquet on May 15 is 900 THB (available at the registration desk)

5.   Additional ticket for lunch on May 15 is 500 THB (available at the registration desk)

6.   Additional ticket for lunch on May 16 is 250 THB (available at the registration desk)
