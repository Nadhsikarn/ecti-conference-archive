Announcement : ECTI-CON 2020 to go virtual  

      Due to circumstances brought on by the COVID 19 situation, the ECTI-CON 2020 committee has been closely monitoring developments of the impact of COVID-19 at local and global levels and has followed the advice provided by the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Government of Thailand.

      Taking into account the rapid increase of cases worldwide and the situation in Thailand is very difficult to assess. Thus the committee finally decided to change the format of accepted papers’ presentation. Following the advice and guidelines from healthcare officials and local authorities, ECTI-CON 2020 will now be held virtually on 24-27 June 2020.

      Accepted papers for the ECTI-CON 2020 will be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore Digital Library after they are presented at the virtual conference. All presentations will be recorded as an evidence to support each paper to be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore. Information and instructions on how to prepare for a virtual presentation will be announced later.

Conference Registration Fee

      As the format of presentations will be changed, we also adjusted the registration fee detailed in registration.html

      We know that some of you are feeling frustrated by missing the wonderful places of Phuket, atmosphere of friendship, the length of time it has taken us to make this decision, but this has not been an easy decision to make. We extend our heartfelt thanks and appreciation to all of our technical community for your understanding and community engagement. Although ECTI-CON 2020 cannot be held physically, the integrity and quality of the research and content will remain and now be experienced in the virtual environment.

     Sinchai Kamolphiwong
     ECTI-CON 2020 General Chair
     25 April 2020

Conference Dates 24-27 June 2020

 

      The 17th International Conference on Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ECTI-CON 2020) is the annual international conference organized by Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ECTI) Association, Thailand. The conference aims to provide an international platform to present technological advances, launch new ideas and showcase research work in the field of electrical engineering, electronics, computer, telecommunications and information technology. Accepted papers will be published in the Proceedings of ECTI-CON 2020 and will be submitted for inclusion in the IEEE Xplore (Conference Record #49241 ISBN: 978-1-7281-6486-1). Acceptance will be based on quality, relevance and originality.

      Papers related to this conference theme include theories, methodologies, and emerging application are encouraged. Contributions covering theoretical developments and practical applications, including but not limited to the following technical areas, are invited:

Devices, Circuit and Systems

Computers

Engineered Mediums/Complex Mediums/Metamaterials

Information Technology

Communication Systems

Controls, Instrumentation and Measurements

Electrical Power Systems

Power Electronics

Photonics

Signal Processing

Artificial Intelligence

Biomedical Engineering and Bio Science

Other Related Areas

 



      High quality papers will be invited to submit an extended version of their work for potential publication in a Special Issue Selected Papers from the 17th International Conference on Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ECTI-CON 2020) of an AEÜ - International Journal of Electronics and Communications (ISSN: 1434-8411), an international peer-reviewed journal on all aspects of design and implementation of electronic devices, circuits, and communication systems published by Elsevier B.V. and indexed by Web of Science (2018 JCR Q2; Impact Factor: 2.853), Scopus, Inspec, etc. The submitted papers must have at least 50% new material from the conference original papers.   Authors should state in “Acknowledgment” part of their journal paper that “A preliminary version of this work has been published in ECTI-CON 2020” and cited therein.".
