      Welcome to the sixth ECTI-CON 2009 in Pataya Thailand.  The ECTI-CON is was originally set up in 2004 and, during the past few years, ECTI conference has achieved steady growth as evident from the support received from the academia and industry.  Last year, ECTI has evolved into the leading international conference in the region and has been listed in the IEEE Xplore.   Our technical program covers variety of topics ranged from electrical power system to information technology.   Each submission was peer reviewed by three or more program committee members.  The techical program will last two days from  Thursday May 7 and Friday May 8, 2009. The papers are grouped into several technical sessions each with a specific technical track.  We have organized three keynote speeches on the current tread on technology and technology management.    These keynotes sessions are conducted by experts from academia, research institute, and government agency from around the globe.  We are hoping that the technical programs of the Conference will stimulate future research in this area.

       The success of this conference required significant effort and dedication on the part of many people. I would like to express my sincere thanks  to the member of organizing committee, advisory board, reviewers, students, and ECTI committee who have offered their efforts along the process.  Finally, we hope that you will find the presentations and discussions  to be a valuable resource in your professional, research, and educational activities . I am looking forward to see you in Pataya, Thailand


Monai Krairiksh
General Chair of ECTI-CON 2009
