      ECTI-CON is the annual international conference organized by Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ECTI) Association, Thailand. The conference aims to be an international forum for presentation of technological advances and research results in the field of electrical engineering, electronics, computer, telecommunications, and information technology. It focuses on the field of Electrical Engineering and Computers. This conference attempts to facilitate research community in the region to exchange their idea and research works. The schedule is opened for plenty of opportunity for informal discussions during breaks, lunches, a welcome reception, and other social event. The ECTI program committees have gathered an excellent technical program for researchers, academics, and practitioners to learn and exchange information on the latest research results in EE, IT, Telecom, Computer Engineering, and Computer Sciences. Additionally, these events serve as the foundation for the improvement of research and education in international level.

       The last year, ECTI 2008 conference was regarded to be the largest EE's related conference yet, with over 10 parallel sessions which covered the whole two days. and representation from many countries.  Accepted papers will be published in theProceedings of ECTI-CON 2009 and will be available via IEEE Xplore. Acceptance will be based on quality, relevance, and originality.
