Ambassador City Jomtien Pattaya Overview :

Ambassador Resort is an ideal place to stay while you can enjoy sun and sand and Gulf of Siam. There is also excitement of surrounding landscape, the beach is ringed with active water sports and you can enjoy the sunseting panoramic view. Not far from peaceful JOMTIEN beach, you can explore the famous city life of Pattaya.
