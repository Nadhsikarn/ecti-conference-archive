Student Travel Grants

Purpose
  The Student Travel Grant aims at providing financial support to students in order to encourage their professional growth as future leaders in the field of electrical engineering, electronics, computer, telecommunications and information technology.

Grant overview
  The grant is meant to support personal attendance to a number of students at the ECTI-CON 2019 conference. Students selected for the grant will receive free registration and 5000THB travel expenses. Grantees need to pay for and make their own accommodation arrangements.

  The exact number of the awards will be determined based on the number of eligible applicants and the availability of funds.

Requirements for Grant consideration
  Reimbursement will occur at the banquet during the conference. Successful applicants must present their papers at the ECTI-CON 2019 Technical Sessions.

Application Procedure
  Students are encouraged to have the applications sent as soon as possible. Applications arriving after the deadline will not be considered. The student travel grant application deadline is May 31, 2019.

 Please submit your paper first (get your paper ID), then download and complete the following application form:
ECTI2019_application form

 Email the application form in PDF format to the Travel Grant Chairs/ Dr. Sarinya Pasakawee as an attachment, with an email subject line: ECTI_GRANT_ [Student’s Full Name].
