Transportation & Venue

Transportation

• If landed at Suvannaphumi Airport
1. Intercity Bus/Van
 At the arriving terminal on the 1st floor, door #8 (in front of the airport cafeteria), buy the ticket at the counter there. The bus number 389 (Suvannaphumi Airport– Pattaya (Jomtien Beach)). Depart from 6.45 am to 21.45 pm (every 45 minutes) cost 134 THB

 

 Get off at the final stop (called “Thep-pra-sit”), then take a blue “Song-Taew” public transport (pick-up with a roof) at this station to the beach in front hotel.
 

2. By Taxi  At the arrivals terminal on the 1st floor, door #4 and #7, the price is on meter display plus 50 THB for service (approx. 1500-2000 THB depends on the traffic).


• If landed at Donmueng Airport It is recommended to get the transportation to Suvannaphumi Airport. - Option 1:  Take the public van (number 555 on the right hand side at Terminal 1) to Suvannaphumi Airport. - Option 2:  Take the bus number 554 at the bus stop in front Donmueng Airport to Suvannaphumi Airport. - Option 3:  Take the airport bus number A1 or A2 at the terminal 1 Door #6-7 to BTS (Mochit), then take BTS to Phyathai station. There is a skywalk connection to the Airport Rail Link which is to Suvannaphumi Airport.


More information on the Public Transportation, please call: 1490 or 0-2134-4099 Or contact the information service at the airport.
