Conference Venue


Novotel Hua Hin - Cha Am Beach Resort & Spa (Formally known as Courtyard by Mariott at Cha-am Beach) 

Due to technical problems, booking through the hotel website is closed at the moment.

For room reservation, please contact  destination.ebc@gmail.comThis e-mail address is being protected from spambots. You need JavaScript enabled to view it


With every room offering a balcony with uninterrupted views over the Gulf Of Thailand, the 243-room Courtyard by Marriott Hua Hin at Cha-am Beach is perfect for families, couples or business meetings. Just over two hours from Bangkok, the resort provides great service, local and international menues and a vibrant destination.The signature MoMo Cafe hosts lunch and dinner, while the Horizon Cafe serves a sumptuous breakfast. MoMo 2 Go provides all-day snack and international teas and coffee, whilst the Havana Lounge serves chilled evening cocktails.Warm friendly service combines wonderfully with the Araya Spa, Kids World play zone the fitness center and large pool.Ten meeting rooms provide the latest audio-visual equipment. The 576sqm Leelawadee Grand Hall can host up to 400 delegates whilst an outdoor area is ideal for team-building activities. Excursions can be organized to places such as the night market for local seafood, King Rama VI's Palace, the Hua Hin Vineyard, the Pala-U Waterfall and golf.
