ISCIT 2024

The 23rd International Symposium on Communications and Information Technologies (ISCIT2024) will be held in Bangkok, Thailand. ISCIT 2024 will seek the technical sponsorship of IEEE. ISCIT2024 provides a forum for researchers, engineers and industry experts to exchange and discuss new ideas, recent development, and breakthroughs in communications and information technologies. ISCIT2024 also offers an exciting social program on the beautiful city of Bangkok.

ISCIT2024 will be organized in hybrid modes: onsite and online. The opening ceremony and keynotes will be available for online participants via video conference. For smoothness, the paper presentations of onsite and online participants will be scheduled in separate sessions. All participants must specify either onsite or online when submitting papers. A special session is an exception. All special session papers must be presented onsite.

Authors can choose to submit full papers (up to 6 pages) or extended abstracts (up to 2 pages). The accepted full papers that are registered and presented will be included in IEEE Xplore, while the accepted extended abstracts will not be included in IEEE Xplore.
