Registration Information

Registration Information:

"IEEE reserves the right to exclude a paper from distribution after the conference, including IEEE Xplore® Digital Library, if the paper is not presented by the author at the conference."
  1. Each accepted paper needs at least 1 regular or ECTI/IEEE Member or ECTI/IEEE Student early-bird registration; otherwise the paper cannot be included in the conference proceedings.
  2. At least one author of each accepted paper MUST present paper in the conference in order for the paper to be included in the proceedings. The proceedings will be available through IEEE Xplore.
  3. Member or Student Member of ECTI or IEEE must declare a valid membership status e.g. a copy of Member or Student Member card or other membership evidences upon registration, where our registration will allow a file download of relevant membership evidences to the system.
  4. The author(s) pays the registration fee by logging into the author account and selecting payment. The author(s) can pay for one accepted paper at a time. (Entering Camera Ready Paper Submission & Author-Presenter Registration Website)
  5. For other authors (not presenting papers) or observers, please enter the registration site here.

Remark:

  1. Regular, ECTI/IEEE Member, ECTI/IEEE Student Member Registration covers Abstract book, CD-ROM, Bag, Souvenir, Coffee-Break, Lunch, Welcome Dinner (15 May), Banquet (16 May).
  2. Accompanying person registration covers Abstract book, CD-ROM, Bag, Souvenir, Coffee-Break, Lunch, Welcome Dinner (15 May), Banquet (16 May). But accompanying person registration cannot cover the inclusion of papers into IEEEXplore.
  3. Additional ticket for welcome dinner on 15 May is is 700 THB (available at the registration desk)
  4. Additional ticket for banquet on 16 May is is 1,000 THB (available at the registration desk)
