To celebrate ECTI-CON reaching its tenth in the series of annual international conference as organized by Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ECTI) Association, Thailand, this year we have introduced a new special industry session at ECTI-CON 2013 with specially selected venue in Krabi Province, one of the most popular nature-lover tourist attraction cities in Thailand. The objective of ECTI-CON special industry sessions is to widen opportunities for local and international industries as well as academia to exchange ideas on active research and development topics that are relevant to Thailand, ASEAN and global contexts. 

Scope of our interests cover (but not limited to) the following themes:

  • Communication and information technology platforms
    for future smart city and smart communities
  •  Future internet technologies
  •  Sustainable energy management systems
  •  Green engineering technologies 
  •  Intelligent devices and mobile computing
  • Application of multimedia and signal processing
    technologies
  • Smart power grid and renewable energy technogies
  • Intelligent transportation systems
  • Trends in Thai, ASEAN and global telecommunication
    and broadcasting industries
