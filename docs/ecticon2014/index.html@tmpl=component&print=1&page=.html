     ECTI-CON 2014 is the eleventh annual international conference organized by Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ECTI) Association, Thailand. The conference aims to be an international forum for presentation of technological advances and research results in the field of electrical engineering, electronics, computer, telecommunications, and information technology. Accepted papers will be published in the Proceedings of ECTI-CON 2014 and will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore. Acceptance will be based on quality, relevance, and originality.
