[28 Apr 2009]

      We will have welcome dinner on May 6 at Sala-sanook-sanan restaurant next  to Alcazar Pattaya.

The conference organizer will prepare BUS to transport between Ambassador Hotel and the restaurant.

The first bus will leave at 17:30 and the second bus will leave at 18:00 and bring us back after the show

After dinner, at 20:00, we provide 200 seats for the Alcazar shows for our participants, please reserve your seats at the registration desk in the afternoon.  The best seats are reserved for ECTI-CON Participants.  You'll love every minute you spend in Alcazar. On stage is a bevy of professional entertainers presenting artistic impersonations through songs and tales of love and adventure with a fantastic sound and light show.

Alcazar Show

One of Thailand's foremost ladyboy cabarets, Alcazar has been a true feast for the eyes for over 20 years. Prepare to be thrilled by 400 dazzling, talented artists and entertainers who will deliver fun-filled performances including comedic impersonations and dramatic numbers.

- Admission fee as mentioned on this program.

      Program Excludes:

- Personal expenses such as drinks, tips, insurance etc.
- Other service not mentioned on this program.
