DETAILED DIRECTION TO ECTI-CON 2016 VENUE
(THE EMPRESS HOTEL)

Depending on the location you arrive at in Chiang Mai, there can be multiple travel options available:

(1) From Chiang Mai Airport ⇒ The Empress Hotel
By Taxi

- Purchase taxi ticket from CHIANGMAI AIRPORT TAXI counter located inside the Domestic Terminal near the exit.

Rate: 160 Baht for regular taxi, 260 Baht for large taxi

- The exit to taxis and limousine cars



Estimated travel time (5.7 km in distance from Chiang Mai Airport ⇒ The Empress Hotel)
  • 18 min on car
  • 22 min on motorcycle
  • 50 min on foot
(2) From Chiang Mai Arcade Bus Terminal ⇒ The Empress Hotel
By Taxi

- Head to the yellow-blue TAXI SERVICE counter (service fee: 150 Baht)



By Red Car

- Head to the RED CAR SERVICE counter (service fee: 120 Baht, 4 passengers max)



Estimated travel time (4.7 km in distance from Chiang Mai Arcade Bus Terminal ⇒ The Empress Hotel)
  • 18 min on car
  • 24 min on motorcycle
  • 55 min on foot
(3) From Chiang Mai Train Station ⇒ The Empress Hotel
By Red Car

- Use red car service in front of the station (service fee: 100 Baht)



Estimated travel time (3.4 km in distance from Chiang Mai Train Station Terminal ⇒ The Empress Hotel)
  • 13 min on car
  • 15 min on motorcycle
  • 34 min on foot
