ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

ICA-SYMP 2027 (IEEE Conference Record Number #71694) is the fifth international symposium organized by Electrical Engineering/Electronics, Computer, Telecommunications, and Information Technology (ECTI) Association, and technically co-sponsored by IEEE Thailand Section, Artificial Intelligence Association of Thailand (AIAT) and Thailand Robotics Society (TRS).

The conference aims to provide an international platform to present technological advances and innovations in intelligent computing and automation.
