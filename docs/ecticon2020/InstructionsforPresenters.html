Instructions for Presenters

  1. Create a Webex account (free) https://www.webex.com/.
  2. Download and Install a Cisco Webex Meeting Desktop App.
    • Sign in your Webex site (https://globalpage-prod.webex.com/signin).
    • Select Download > Cisco Webex Meeting Desktop App > Download.
  3. You have to upload your presentation (.ppt, pptx or pdf) to our website, which will be available during  1 - 17 June 2020.
     ECTI-CON 2020 Presentation Upload

  4. We will send the invitation e-mail to join ECTI-CON 2020 virtual conference. Note: We will use the e-mail, which you filled in the registration system.
  5. In each session, you should join the session 20 minutes before starting the session.
** For further information, please refer to our instructions at Cisco Webex Guide for ECTI-CON 2020.
Cisco Webex Guide for ECTI-CON 2020.
Presentation Guidelines

          Oral presenters will have 15 minutes to present, followed by 5 minutes of question and answer. The timing will be strict, and session chairs will be requested to stop speakers exceeding the 20-minute presentation slot.

          As a general guideline you should calculate 1-2 minutes per slide, so for a 15-minute presentation, more than 15 slides is often too much. Try not to overload your slides with information, keep it brief and easy to follow. As the audiences will see your presentation through the Webex application, please ensure that the text and figures are visible enough to read.
